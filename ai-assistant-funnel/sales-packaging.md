# WASCIK Customer Packaging Guide

This module can be sold as a reusable service rather than a one-off chatbot build.

## Suggested service tiers

### 1. AI Website Representative
Best for local service businesses.

Includes:
- branded on-page representative
- approved FAQ/service knowledge
- lead qualification
- contact/handoff actions
- page-aware behavior

### 2. AI Shopping Guide
Best for affiliate sites, retailers, product catalogs, and e-commerce.

Includes:
- normalized product catalog
- intent-based recommendations
- product comparison support
- merchant/category scoping
- disclosure-aware outbound links

### 3. Branded Digital Ambassador
Adds a higher-end visual experience.

Includes:
- custom avatar/persona
- branded speech-bubble interface
- industry-specific greeting and behavior
- optional voice and animation integrations

### 4. Authorized Digital Likeness
Premium service using an owner or employee likeness or voice with explicit permission.

Includes:
- consent workflow
- custom visual/voice assets
- restricted approved business knowledge
- disclosure and replacement controls

### 5. Private Owner AI Studio
Internal tool for the customer or business owner.

Possible features:
- social captions
- short-form video scripts
- ad concepts
- content batches
- campaign planning
- product/service promotion drafts

Keep this workspace authenticated and separate from the public assistant.

## Recurring revenue opportunities

WASCIK can charge separately for:
- initial setup/customization
- monthly AI usage/hosting
- knowledge-base maintenance
- catalog updates
- CRM or booking integrations
- analytics/reporting
- avatar upgrades
- voice capability
- multilingual support
- priority support

## Implementation principle

Maintain one reusable engine and configure it per customer. Avoid copying and permanently forking the whole implementation for every website unless a customer has genuinely unique requirements.
