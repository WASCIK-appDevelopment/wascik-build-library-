# Affiliate Partnership Intake and Promotion Controls

A reusable workflow for evaluating, negotiating, testing, accepting, publishing, and advertising an affiliate partnership.

## Lifecycle

1. Invited
2. Terms retrieved
3. Business and product evaluated
4. Conditions or questions sent
5. Written response received
6. Product and tracking tested
7. Network invitation accepted
8. Brand enabled in the private console
9. Products approved for publication
10. Promotion channels enabled according to contract
11. Performance and term changes monitored

Do not collapse these states. An email expressing conditional interest is not network acceptance. Network acceptance is not product approval. Product approval is not permission for every advertising channel. Affiliate acceptance is not permission for a technical integration or strategic partnership.

## Contract record

Capture:

- advertiser legal entity and support contact
- network, merchant/program identity, currency, and geography
- commission basis, rates, tiers, and duration
- monthly versus annual subscription treatment
- cookie or attribution window
- cancellation, reactivation, refund, reversal, and lock/payment rules
- permitted editorial, organic social, email, coupon, influencer, retargeting, display, DSP, paid social, paid search, and shopping-ad channels
- trademark, ad-copy, display-URL, coupon-code, creative-modification, and disclosure restrictions
- notice period for term changes
- termination and rapid-disable requirements

When a structured network field conflicts with bespoke advertiser terms, apply the more restrictive rule until the advertiser confirms otherwise in writing.

## SaaS and AI-product diligence

Before recommending a subscription or AI service, confirm:

- complimentary or controlled testing access
- included features and test duration
- agency use for paying-client work
- ownership of generated assets
- client commercial-use rights
- treatment of uploaded client images and confidential materials
- AI-training use or opt-out
- retention and deletion process
- privacy-policy location
- product quality, reliability, export quality, support, and billing behavior
- launch age and independent review depth

## Promotion-policy enforcement

Store promotion permissions as structured data used by the ad workflow. Default an unknown channel to blocked or needs written approval. Examples include:

- organic social allowed while paid social is blocked
- retargeting blocked without written approval
- DSP promotion requires advance approval
- paid search earns zero commission or is prohibited
- trademark bidding, shopping ads, coupon language, or altered creative prohibited

The ad generator should explain why a channel is unavailable and identify the required approval record. It must not rely on an operator remembering contract text.

## Acceptance gate

Accept and advertise only after the controlling questions are answered, testing passes, and the owner explicitly approves. Preserve the source terms and written permissions in the customer project, not this reusable library.
