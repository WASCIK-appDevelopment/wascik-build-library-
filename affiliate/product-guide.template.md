## Reader-first outline

1. Plain-English product summary
2. Who the product may suit
3. Important features
4. Limitations and buying considerations
5. Setup or compatibility information
6. Frequently asked questions
7. Current merchant link and disclosure

## Evidence rules

- Label merchant claims as merchant claims.
- Never describe research as personal testing.
- Do not copy merchant descriptions wholesale.
- Verify model names, specifications, availability, and links.
- Include original observations only when the author has actually made them.
- Keep price and promotion claims time-stamped or direct readers to the merchant's current listing.

## Standard disclosure

This page may contain affiliate links. If a reader clicks one and makes a qualifying purchase, the publisher may earn a commission at no additional cost to the reader.
