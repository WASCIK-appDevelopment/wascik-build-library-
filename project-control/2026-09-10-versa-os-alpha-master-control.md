## 1. Product identity

The product is now **Versa OS**. It should no longer be treated or described internally as merely an owner console. WASCIK's current internal implementation is the first real operating installation of a broader configurable business operating system.

The controlling Versa identity is the approved V-Core Portal / Infinite-V hybrid visual system:

- ram-horn / infinite-V geometry;
- central luminous blue core;
- blue/cyan and gold illuminated metallic surfaces;
- dark cinematic architectural environment;
- premium liquid-glass interface language.

The high-fidelity approved portal artwork is the controlling portal presentation. Generic CSS/SVG recreations are not acceptable substitutes where the approved artwork is intended.

## 8. Alpha OS identity and interaction

Alpha is the intelligence layer inside Versa and future WASCIK platforms.

The earlier animated-neuron concept has been superseded visually. The controlling Alpha identity is now a **luminous blue energy orb** derived from the core aesthetic used in the Versa V.

Controlling states:

- **Idle:** small blue orb.
- **Listening:** enlarged orb with purple/violet voice-reactive pulsing or waveform activity.
- **Thinking:** enlarged orb with blue/cyan pulsing/energy activity.
- **Response complete:** orb contracts back to idle.

The dedicated Alpha conversation screen should use a solid black background, persistent typing input, send control, and `+` attachment position.

Voice interaction target:

- tap Alpha to begin listening;
- tap again to stop and submit;
- transition directly into thinking state;
- return to compact state when the response is ready.

## 9. Alpha capability boundary and roadmap

Current/near-term Alpha is not autonomous. The safe progression is:

1. converse, reason, explain, brainstorm, recommend;
2. read selected Versa data;
3. propose actions without executing them;
4. execute selected actions only after explicit confirmation;
5. add web search, website interaction, file analysis, external tools/APIs, and cross-module workflows;
6. eventually support broader automation under auditable permissions and organization rules.

Alpha should ultimately behave as one intelligence layer with separate contexts and permissions, not unrelated copies of a chatbot scattered across products.

Potential connected platforms include:

- Versa OS;

- future financial tools/platforms;
- intellectual-property management tools;
- WASCIK Media Studio / media-production systems;
- future WASCIK products.

Alpha should eventually be capable of authorized cross-platform reasoning and communication while respecting strict data and action boundaries for each product and organization.

## 11. Versa product architecture

Versa should be designed in three reusable layers.

### Versa Core

Common to every installation:

- V-Core Portal;
- authentication;
- main Versa workspace;
- navigation;
- Settings;
- Alpha OS;
- notifications;
- files/documents;
- search;
- account/user/security controls;
- shared integration infrastructure.

### Business Core

Modules useful to many organizations:

- CRM/customers;
- leads;
- estimates/quotes;
- invoices;
- purchase orders;
- expenses;
- projects;
- documents;
- calendar/tasks;
- communications;
- advertising/marketing;
- reporting/analytics;
- tax-document organization;
- vendor/employee records;
- payments/integrations where appropriate.

Not every business has to enable every Business Core module.

### Industry Workspace

A configurable business-specific dashboard and function set layered on top of the shared core.

Examples:

- construction: jobs, estimates, subcontractors, materials, change orders, inspections;
- salon: clients, appointments, services, staff, inventory, rebooking;
- church/nonprofit: members, ministries, events, giving, volunteers, communications;
- authors/publishers: manuscripts, books, canon/research, publishing, marketing, royalties, media;
- media/production: productions, assets, talent, schedules, budgets, distribution;
- WASCIK app/web business: Leads, Social & Ads, Affiliate Search, Brand Discovery, Published Products, Portfolio, analytics, and related modules.

The current WASCIK Versa installation should be treated as the first specialized Industry Workspace and as a flagship portfolio demonstration.

A few thousand paying business accounts at meaningful recurring revenue can already support a substantial software company. The correct near-term sequence is to prove value with early users, then scale what retains customers.
