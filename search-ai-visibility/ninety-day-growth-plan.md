# Ninety-Day Search and AI Visibility Plan

Adapt the sequence to verified project conditions. Do not repeat completed setup as new work.

## Days 1-30 - discovery, measurement, and conversion

- verify crawler access, sitemap, canonicals, metadata, and structured data
- verify search-engine ownership and indexing state
- connect measurement and genuine lead events
- complete one short service-page-to-form-to-thank-you conversion path
- correct local profile identity and service-area settings
- establish branded and non-branded search baselines
- establish AI-answer benchmark prompts
- publish or substantially improve the two highest-value service pages
- begin requesting genuine reviews

## Days 31-60 - content and authority

- publish one useful original guide, FAQ expansion, case study, or project breakdown per week
- build accurate local citations
- improve thin pages rather than multiplying them
- add named authorship, evidence, update dates, and internal links
- pursue relevant partnerships and third-party mentions
- compare Search Console queries with the original opportunity map
- repeat the fixed AI-answer benchmark

## Days 61-90 - optimization and packaging

- compare qualified leads by source
- improve calls to action and form completion
- update pages from actual query and conversion evidence
- expand topic or location coverage only where real demand and distinct value exist
- document successful procedures as reusable modules
- decide whether the process is reliable enough to sell as a recurring growth service

## Weekly scorecard

Record:

- deployment and verification changes
- indexed pages and exclusions
- branded impressions and clicks
- non-branded local impressions and clicks
- average position for priority query groups
- calls, emails, forms, and qualified leads
- local-profile activity
- new reviews and citations
- AI-answer mentions and citations
- actions completed
- next test and responsible owner

## Guardrails

- no guaranteed rankings, traffic, leads, sales, reviews, or approvals
- no mass-produced near-duplicate pages
- no private address disclosure
- no fake proof or unsupported schema
- no automatic production publishing outside the approved project rule
