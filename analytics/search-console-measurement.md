# Search Console Measurement Plan

Use this after the adopting website is deployed, verified, and submitted to Google Search Console.

## Baseline measures

- Valid indexed pages
- Pages excluded from indexing and the stated reason
- Search impressions
- Search clicks
- Click-through rate
- Average position
- Queries producing impressions
- Landing pages producing impressions
- Branded versus non-branded searches
- Country and device mix

## Business outcomes

Search metrics are leading indicators. Also track:

- Contact-form submissions
- Phone-button taps
- Email-button taps
- Qualified leads
- Affiliate-link clicks
- Confirmed affiliate conversions
- Revenue where reliable attribution exists

## Review cadence

- Weekly: indexing errors, sudden traffic loss, broken pages
- Monthly: query and landing-page opportunities
- Quarterly: content consolidation, decay, conversion performance, and strategy

Do not make a ranking decision from a few days of data. Record the comparison period, query filters, page filters, and any major site changes.
