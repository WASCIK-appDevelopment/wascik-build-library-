# Growth Engine

Reusable workflows for helping a website become discoverable, useful, measurable, and continuously improvable.

## Core cycle

1. Audit crawlability, metadata, structure, performance, accessibility, and content.
2. Record verified business facts and publishing constraints.
3. Find search opportunities and score them by value, relevance, attainability, and readiness.
4. Prepare controlled content or technical changes.
5. Require owner review for major claims and publishing.
6. Preview, validate, and publish deliberately.
7. Measure impressions, clicks, leads, sales, and content decay.
8. Feed results into the next prioritized work list.

## Boundaries

- Do not guarantee rankings.
- Do not mass-produce repetitive search pages.
- Do not invent business facts, prices, product specifications, reviews, or firsthand experience.
- Keep production publishing behind explicit approval.
- Store company-specific data in the adopting project's private configuration, not in this reusable library.
